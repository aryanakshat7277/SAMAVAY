### Core Visual Design System (Sovereign Authority Theme)

- **Color Harmony**:
  - `gov-950` (`#092119` Deep Midnight Pine) & `gov-700` (`#1B5C43` Sovereign Forest Emerald)
  - `saffron-500/600` (`#D97706` Antique Ochre / Gold Accent)
  - `sandstone-100` (`#FAF8F5` Warm Porcelain Canvas)
  - Pure `stone-*` neutral hierarchy eliminating all legacy `slate-*` and generic `blue-*` classes.
- **Typography**: Authentic government serif headings (`font-serif` with Noto Serif & Georgia fallbacks) paired with clean geometric Sans for content and Monospace for cryptographic hashes and telemetry latencies.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Dynamic document scroll progress bar, live SVG data packet animations (`dashFlow`), pulsing sovereign node rings, staggered list/grid entrances (`animate-stagger-in`), and hover lifts.
