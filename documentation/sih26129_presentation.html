02 / PROBLEM ANATOMY SIH26129

The Cost of Fragmented Digital Governance

Digital platforms exist in abundance, but operate as disconnected data silos.

👤 Citizen Experience Bottlenecks

  • ❌4+ Portals to Visit: Separate logins for Bhoomi, SARATHI, e-NagarPalika & PFMS.
  • ❌72% Repeated Data Entry: Re-uploading Aadhaar, PAN, and land titles already held by the state.
  • ❌14–21 Days Average Delay: Long delays due to manual inter-departmental inquiries.
  • ❌High Drop-off Rates: 40%+ citizens abandon applications due to document friction.

🏛️ Administrative & Governance Challenges

  • ⚠️No Automated Inter-System Pipes: Departments cannot safely verify data in real time.
  • ⚠️High Verification Cost: ₹180+ spent per application on physical inspections.
  • ⚠️Risk of Forged Documents: Manual paper uploads allow counterfeit certificates.
  • ⚠️Zero Cross-System Observability: Administrators lack unified SLA and packet monitoring.
Root Cause: Not a failure of digitization, but an absence of a consent-governed semantic interoperability mesh. Slide 2 of 12

Stateless Exchange
