The Problem: Departmental Data Silos

Digital government platforms exist in abundance, but operate in complete isolation.

Citizen Experience Bottlenecks

  • ❌4+ Portals to Visit: Citizens must juggle separate logins for Bhoomi, SARATHI, e-NagarPalika & PFMS.
  • ❌70% Repeated Data Entry: Re-uploading Aadhaar, PAN, and land titles already owned by the state.
  • ❌14–21 Days Average Delay: Long delays due to manual inter-departmental inquiries.
